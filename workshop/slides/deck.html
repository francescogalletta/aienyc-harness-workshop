Consistency

🧮20 min

1

2 · Consistency

WHY

Write the calculation once. Run it the same way every time.

The model picks the tool. It never does the arithmetic.

2 · Consistency

WITHOUT IT

New code for every question.

Same question, different answer.

Keep the trail: where each thing came from, and how far to trust it.

4 · Accountability

4

Accountability

🙋25 min

Ask only where a person must decide.

3

The evidence is what you decide with.

4 · Accountability

WHY

Agents face no real-world consequences. You do.

That is your leverage. Keep it.

4 · Accountability

Reduce ambiguity. Do not remove it.

The judgement calls stay with you. The harness makes them easy to make.

4 · Accountability

WITHOUT IT

“IDK, the stochastic machine said it was fine.”

Approving every calculation.

Reviewing every PR your agent ships. lying, or doing it badly

4 · Accountability

IN PRACTICE

Few gates. High signal.

Two plausible readings that conflict: stop and ask.

An assumption: run it, and mark it.

Arithmetic: never ask.

4 · Accountability

Verification

🔍12 min

A reviewer challenges the plan.

4

Each challenge is yours to use or dismiss.

5 · Verification

WHY

Experts do not check every cell.

They know where it usually goes wrong.

And roughly what the answer should look like.

5 · Verification

YOU

thin slices of your own experience

THE MODEL

everyone's experience, averaged

Its “probably fine” may look nothing like yours.

5 · Verification

WITHOUT IT

Checking everything, line by line.

Or checking nothing.

No sense of what “about right” looks like.

5 · Verification

EXAMPLES

Is the guest count really settled?

Is the price per guest all in?

Will each payment be covered by its date?

Each challenge is yours to use or dismiss.
